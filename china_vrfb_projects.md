# Vanadium Redox Flow Battery (VRFB) Investment Projects in China

*Last Updated: December 2024*

## Executive Summary

China dominates the global vanadium redox flow battery market, accounting for 49.6% of market share in 2023. In 2024, VRFB grid-connected projects increased by over 600% compared to 2023, with 16 projects totaling approximately 440MW/1,742MWh connected to the grid.

**Key Statistics (2024):**
- Grid-connected projects: ~440MW/1,742MWh
- Under construction: ~745MW/3,200MWh
- Planned/announced: ~2,124MW/8,886MWh
- Total industry investment planned: >33.7 billion RMB
- Production capacity target: 48 GWh
